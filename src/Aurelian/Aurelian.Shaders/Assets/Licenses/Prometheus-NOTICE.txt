docs/Aurelian/compute-math-provenance.json.

Aurelian changes: immutable typed parameter transport; fixed shared-memory
arrays and checked synchronization; portable tree reductions instead of
subgroup-width assumptions; explicit 16-element FP32 K dispatches; bounded
device-resident plans; symmetric NHWC im2col; host-validated optional KHR
cooperative tuple with packed-half ingress; independent native CPU oracles.
The Prometheus reactor, batch policy engine, model weights and model loaders
are not copied or linked.
